--------------------------------------------------

2. Read src/index.css and list every CSS custom property you find.
   Note the exact variable names. You will use only these in Phase 2.

   Confirm the exact path strings for the login and register routes.
   Write them down. Do not guess or invent route paths.

--------------------------------------------------
COPY FOR PHASE 1
--------------------------------------------------

Learning loop step descriptions:
Hear it — Meet useful Spanish in small pieces.
Repeat it — Echo sounds, words, and phrases.
Build it — Create sentences step by step.
Use it — Practice in context through reading and conversation.
Remember it — Recall language until it starts to come back naturally.

Practice variety body:
Murcielingo gives you different ways to meet the same language again:
listen, repeat, choose, build, read, speak, and recall.
So when one practice is done, another useful way to keep learning
can begin.

Practice mode descriptions:
Echo Practice: Listen, repeat, and recall Spanish until it starts
to stick.
Sentence Builder: Build useful sentences step by step.
Flashcards: Meet important words again at the right moment.
Vocabulary: Practice words in context, not just as lists.
Grammar: Understand patterns through simple examples.
Reading: Read short Spanish texts with support.
Conversation: Practice everyday Spanish in small dialogues.
Pronunciation: Train sounds, rhythm, and confidence.

Mobile body:
Murcielingo is being prepared for mobile, so your Spanish practice
can follow you through the day.
