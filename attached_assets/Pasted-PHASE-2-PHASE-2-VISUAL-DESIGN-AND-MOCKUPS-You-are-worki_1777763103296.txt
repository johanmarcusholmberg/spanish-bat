--------------------------------------------------

The visual style must be:

- Language-learning focused, not SaaS/dashboard

- Peach/coral for primary CTA buttons
- Muted mint for highlights and accents
- Soft rounded cards (existing shadcn/ui Card component)
- Large friendly typography (Nunito or Quicksand if already loaded)
- Subtle shadows and gentle hover states

Avoid:
- Cold blue SaaS design
- Corporate dashboard aesthetics
- Dense grids
- Aggressive animations
- Any numbers, fake charts, or fake statistics
- Fake testimonials or invented user quotes
